# Introduction

## TREE: The token of Treehouse Protocol

TREE is the native utility and governance token of the Treehouse Protocol. It is designed to coordinate incentives across stakeholders—including users, Panelists, Delegators, and developers—within the Decentralized Offered Rate (DOR) ecosystem. It plays a critical role in securing the protocol, governing system parameters, and enabling ecosystem growth through staking, fee routing, and incentive distribution.

# Token Details


# Utility

The TREE token serves as a medium of exchange for the Treehouse ecosystem, driving its growth, enabling decentralized governance, and incentivizing positive behaviors among participants.

The utilities for the TREE token include:<br>

1. **TREE Staking:** TREE holders can delegate TREE to a whitelisted Panelist on DOR, earning a share of rewards based on that Panelist’s prediction accuracy, while contributing to the security of DOR’s rate-setting process.<br>
2. **Querying Fees:** On-chain smart contracts and enterprises that reference DOR data for product development or settlement purposes pay a query fee in TREE to Treehouse stakeholders. This mechanism generates revenue for the ecosystem and ensures the fair utilization of DOR data.<br>
3. **Panelist Staking:** To ensure the accuracy of Panelists' rate submissions and align their interests with the protocol, Panelists must stake TREE or tAssets to participate in the DOR rate-setting process.<br>
4. **Consensus Payouts:** TREE tokens are distributed to Panelists and Delegators of Treehouse-operated DORs for each observation period based on the prediction accuracy of Panelists. This incentivizes accurate prediction and active participation in the DOR consensus mechanism, ensuring the reliability of DOR-derived data streams.<br>
5. **Governance:** TREE holders are granted protocol governance rights, enabling them to participate in decision-making processes that affect the protocol’s parameters and design. This active involvement empowers TREE holders to shape the future growth and development of the Treehouse ecosystem, ensuring alignment with community interests.<br>
6. **DAO Grants:** The Treehouse DAO allocates TREE to fund strategic partnerships and ecosystem initiatives, incentivizing the development of products built on the Treehouse ecosystem. These grants encourage innovation, accelerate product development, and expand the adoption of DOR across digital assets.

#### Community Rewards (20%)

20% of TREE is allocated to incentivize long-term alignment among Treehouse users. These rewards are distributed to participants who stake, govern, or actively engage with protocol activities.

#### Strategic Investors (17.5%)

Allocated to early backers and strategic partners who provided the financial and infrastructural support necessary to bootstrap Treehouse’s development. Their ongoing involvement helps accelerate product rollout, liquidity provisioning, and institutional adoption. All tokens in this category follow a vesting schedule to ensure long-term alignment.

#### Team (12.5%)

This allocation compensates the core team responsible for building the foundational architecture of Treehouse and its early products. Tokens are subject to a multi-year vesting schedule to incentivize continued commitment and protocol stewardship.

#### Treasury (12.5%)

The DAO-controlled treasury receives 12.5% of the TREE supply. These tokens are earmarked for future ecosystem needs, including protocol upgrades, partnerships, and liquidity support. Treasury usage is governed through on-chain voting and serves as a reserve for long-term protocol sustainability.

#### Community Airdrop (10%)

One-time distribution of TREE to early contributors, testers, and protocol participants to democratize ownership. This airdrop recognizes the grassroots efforts of Treehouse’s early believers and sets the stage for broader community governance participation.

#### Ecosystem Fund (10%)

A long-term fund dedicated to builders, developers, and ecosystem partners who expand the DOR standard and Treehouse product suite. The Ecosystem Fund supports grants, integrations, hackathons, and new on-chain products that leverage Treehouse infrastructure.

#### Core Contributors (5%)

Allocated to individuals and entities who played a critical role in protocol design, research, or infrastructure development. This allocation is governed separately from the Team allocation and focuses on long-term contributors beyond the core team.

#### Exchange Partnerships (3.75%)

Tokens reserved for the exchanges to bootstrap community interest, and cultivate alignment between the Treehouse and their ecosystems. This allocation supports awareness, accessibility, and early liquidity.

#### Future Airdrops (5.75%)

Reserved for post-launch growth campaigns, protocol expansions, and strategic community-building efforts. This allocation enables flexibility to reward participation and drive adoption beyond the initial airdrop phase.

#### Liquidity Provision (3%)

Allocated to seed on-chain liquidity pools and ensure sufficient depth for TREE across key trading venues. These tokens are used to reduce slippage, maintain stable price discovery, and support early token utility.

# Eligibility Criteria

This page outlines the requirements you must meet to participate in our Gaia event.

Snapshot was taken on on 29 May 2025, 00:00 UTC.

To be eligible for the TREE airdrop during the Gaia event, wallets must meet a minimum threshold of 100 Nuts. This ensures that only wallets with a meaningful level of engagement with the Treehouse ecosystem are considered.

We conducted a rigorous review of all participant activity and implemented multiple sybil-resistance filters to eliminate low-effort, botted, or exploitative behaviors. This included pattern analysis, clustering detection, and behavioral heuristics to ensure that only real users and contributors made it through. More details below.

Our goal is to reward genuine participation while protecting the interests of the broader community.

## GoNuts

GoNuts allocations are determined by each wallet’s total Nuts balance, which serves as a proxy for genuine engagement across the Treehouse Protocol.&#x20;

Nuts were earned through both protocol usage and ecosystem participation, including:

* tAsset Holdings: e.g., tETH, gtETH, and Treehouse-integrated vaults (e.g., cmETH)
* Campaign Participation: e.g., *Into the Arb*, *8 Days of 1888*, *Treehouse Games Room*
* Badge Ownership: Season 1 badge milestones were counted toward total Nuts
* Referrals: Users earned bonuses based on Nuts generated by referred participants and their portfolio

To preserve fairness:

* Wallets with exceptionally high balances are placed in the Vested program, subject to a linear release of their Treasury Bonus over time.
* All other eligible wallets are part of the Non-Vested program, receiving their allocation at TGE.

Refer to [Types of Vesting Programs](/protocol/tree-token/airdrop-details/types-of-vesting-programs) for more information on Non-Vested and Vested programs.

### Allocation Mechanism

To ensure a fair and meaningful distribution of TREE, we adopted a progressive allocation model rather than applying a flat conversion rate across all wallets.

This approach allows us to:

* Reward authentic, long-term users of Treehouse
* Prevent airdrop farming and manipulative behavior
* Avoid concentration of supply in a small number of large wallets

## Treehouse Squirrel Council (TSC) NFT Allocations

Holders of Treehouse Squirrel Council (TSC) NFTs are entitled to a dedicated TREE allocation, separate from the Nuts-based airdrop.

* Each TSC NFT grants the holder the right to claim 500 TREE.
* Each NFT can only be used once for claiming; once redeemed, it cannot be used again.
* Eligibility is determined at the time of claim, based on current on-chain ownership. Only the wallet holding the NFT at the time of claim is eligible.
* The claim window is open for 3 months from the TGE. Any unclaimed TREE from the NFT allocation after this period will be returned to the protocol treasury.

This mechanism ensures that rewards are tied directly to live ownership and promotes fair distribution within the TSC community.

## Sybil

To preserve the integrity of our airdrop and reward programs, Treehouse enforces robust sybil resistance measures. Wallets exhibiting manipulative or inauthentic behavior are excluded from receiving TREE allocations.

#### What is a Sybil Account?

A sybil account refers to a wallet—or a coordinated cluster of wallets—controlled by a single actor attempting to mimic multiple independent participants. This behavior can distort engagement metrics, inflate allocations, and undermine the fairness of distribution campaigns.

#### Exclusion Criteria

The following categories of behavior were evaluated and excluded:

* **Inorganic Activity:** Wallet clustering, transaction loops, and other suspicious patterns indicative of automation or coordination.
* **Campaign Tourists:** Wallets that participated only briefly or exited immediately after Season 1, showing no sustained engagement with the ecosystem.
* **Exploitive Strategies**: Behaviors that gamed reward mechanics without contributing to long-term protocol growth or community development.

Note: We do not disclose exact detection methods or heuristics to prevent adversarial gaming. Our guiding principle is simple: only authentic and sustained participation deserves to be rewarded.

# Types of Vesting Programs

To balance immediate access with long-term alignment, Treehouse introduces two airdrop claim pathways: the Non-Vested Program and the Vested Program. Each wallet is assigned to one of these programs based on its total Nuts balance at the time of the airdrop snapshot.

Your assigned program determines how and when you can claim your:

* **Base Allocation** — your core TREE entitlement
* **Treasury Bonus** — additional TREE granted as additional rewards

<figure><img src="/files/PRS5IgXJHQd8q0K6QzZp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Non-Vested Program

**Targeted at active contributors with moderate Nuts balances.**

* 100% of the Base Allocation is immediately claimable at TGE.
* 100% of the Treasury Bonus is also claimable at TGE.
* Eligible for direct staking into Pre-Deposit Vaults during the claim process.
* Claim window is open for 3 months from TGE.
  * Unclaimed tokens after this period will be returned to the Treehouse Treasury.
* Eligible for a 50% Universal Buff in GoNuts Season 2, provided the user stakes at least 50% of their total allocation during the claim process.

## Vested Program

**Designed for top contributors with high Nuts balances to align long-term incentives.**

* 100% of the Base Allocation is immediately claimable at TGE.
* Treasury Bonus starts accruing daily and linearly over 12 months if the Base Allocation remains unclaimed.&#x20;
* Claiming the Base Allocation early ends the vesting period.
  * Upon claiming, users receive the Base Allocation and the accrued portion of the Treasury Bonus.
* Total claim window is 15 months from TGE.
  * Includes a 12-month vesting period and a 3-month grace period for claiming.
  * Any unclaimed tokens after this window will return to the Treehouse Treasury.
* Automatically eligible for a 50% Universal Buff in GoNuts Season 2, provided the Base Allocation remains unclaimed throughout the campaign period.

## FAQ

> A: All eligible airdrop participants receive a 100% Base Allocation along with a 100% Treasury Bonus from Treehouse as an appreciation for their support.&#x20;
>
> * Non-Vested participants: Both Base Allocation and Treasury Bonus can be claimed immediately.
> * Vested participants: The Treasury Bonus accrues linearly over 12 months, provided they do not claim early.

# Eligibility Criteria

A wallet qualifies for the airdrop if it meets **any** of the following:

* Has at least **500 Nuts**&#x20;
* Held at least one Treehouse Squirrel Council NFT at the snapshot block
* Voted on at least one Treehouse governance proposal on Snapshot (`treehouse.eth`) during Season 2.

A wallet's total allocation is the sum of all [tracks](/protocol/tree-token/airdrop-details-s2/eligibility-tracks) it qualifies for:

```
Grand Total TREE = Points Allocation + Governance Allocation + NFT Allocation
```

Where:

* **Points Allocation** = 100 TREE + Pro-rata Share
* **Governance Allocation** = 10,000 TREE&#x20;
* **Treehouse Squirrel Council Allocation** = 10,000 TREE × NFT count


# Eligibility Tracks

**23,100,000 TREE** has been distributed across three independent eligibility tracks. A single wallet can qualify for **one, two, or all three** tracks.

| Track                                                                                                                   | Based On                          | Allocation Logic                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| [**GoNuts**](/protocol/tree-token/airdrop-details-s2/eligibility-tracks/gonuts-s2)                                      | Accumulated Nuts during S2        | Pro-rata share of the Points Pool   |
| [**Governance**](/protocol/tree-token/airdrop-details-s2/eligibility-tracks/governance)                                 | Voting in Treehouse governance    | Flat allocation per eligible wallet |
| [**Treehouse Squirrel Council**](/protocol/tree-token/airdrop-details-s2/eligibility-tracks/treehouse-squirrel-council) | Squirrel Council NFTs at snapshot | Flat allocation per NFT held        |

All eligibility is determined at the snapshot block: **Ethereum mainnet at 25 April 2026, 0200 UTC**.


# GoNuts S2

Your **Total Nuts** determine your pro-rata share of the Nuts Pool.

#### 1. Total Nuts Formula

```
Total Nuts = (Base Nuts + Quest Bonus + Badge Bonus) × Stage Multiplier
```

Each component is explained below.

#### 2. Base Nuts

Your **Base Nuts** are the total Nuts accrued through protocol activity and holdings throughout GoNuts Season 2, up to the snapshot block.&#x20;

This is the running total seen on the dashboard throughout the season.

#### 3. Quest Bonus

Every quest completed during S2 is worth a flat amount of **100 Nuts**.

```
Quest Bonus = 100 × (number of quests completed)
```

#### 4. Badge Bonus

Badges are grouped into three rarity tiers. Each tier has a fixed Nuts value:

| Badge Tier | Nuts per Badge |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| Tier 1     | 500            |
| Tier 2     | 1,000          |
| Tier 3     | 1,500          |

Full badge-to-tier mapping:

| Badge                | Tier | Nuts  |
| -------------------- | ---- | ----- |
| Certification Badge  | 3    | 1,500 |
| Eden Tier Badge      | 3    | 1,500 |
| Full Moon Badge      | 3    | 1,500 |
| Golden Branch Badge  | 3    | 1,500 |
| Baseball Glove Badge | 2    | 1,000 |
| Half Moon Badge      | 2    | 1,000 |
| Ski Goggles Badge    | 2    | 1,000 |
| Crescent Moon Badge  | 1    | 500   |
| Honey Pot Badge      | 1    | 500   |
| Season 1 Medal       | 1    | 500   |

```
Badge Bonus = Σ (Nuts value of each badge held)
```

#### 5. Stage Multiplier

Your stage applies a percentage bonus on top of the sum of Base Nuts + Quest Bonus + Badge Bonus.&#x20;

| Stage    | Bonus | Multiplier Applied |
| -------- | ----- | ------------------ |
| Seed     | 0%    | 1.00×              |
| Sapling  | +10%  | 1.10×              |
| Willow   | +15%  | 1.15×              |
| Oak      | +20%  | 1.20×              |
| Mahogany | +25%  | 1.25×              |
| Eden     | +30%  | 1.30×              |

#### 6. Total Nuts to TREE Conversion

Once every wallet's Total Nuts are computed, TREE is distributed from the **Nuts Pool** — the portion of 23.1M TREE remaining after the Governance and NFT tracks are funded.

Each eligible wallet receives:

```
TREE Allocation = Minimum Floor + Pro-rata Share

Minimum Floor   = 100 TREE (guaranteed for every nuts-eligible wallet)
Pro-rata Share  = (your Total Nuts / all Total Nuts) × (Nuts Pool − total minimums)
```

This means **every wallet with ≥500 Total Nuts is guaranteed at least 100 TREE**, and the rest is distributed linearly in proportion to your share of total Total Nuts across all eligible wallets.


# Governance

Any wallet that voted on **at least one** Treehouse governance proposal via Snapshot (`treehouse.eth`) during Season 2 receives a flat allocation.

| Criterion                       | Allocation             |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Voted on ≥1 governance proposal | **10,000 TREE** (flat) |

This is binary — voting on one proposal or voting on every proposal both yield the same 10,000 TREE.&#x20;

The governance allocation stacks on top of any GoNuts or NFT allocation the wallet is also entitled to.


# Treehouse Squirrel Council

Wallets holding Treehouse Squirrel Council NFTs at the snapshot block receive a flat allocation **per NFT**.

| Criterion                                  | Allocation              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Each Squirrel Council NFT held at snapshot | **10,000 TREE** per NFT |

Allocation scales linearly — holding 5 NFTs gives 50,000 TREE, 15 NFTs gives 150,000 TREE.&#x20;

The NFT allocation stacks on top of any GoNuts or Governance allocation.

NFT ownership is determined on-chain from transfer event history up to the snapshot block.


# Worked Example

Consider a wallet with the following S2 activity:

| Attribute             | Value                                              |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Base Nuts             | 12,000                                             |


**Step 2 — Badge Bonus**

```

```

**Step 3 — Subtotal**

```
12,000 + 800 + 3,000 = 15,800 Nuts
```

**Step 4 — Stage Multiplier (Oak = 1.20×)**

```
Final Nuts = 15,800 × 1.20 = 18,960
```

**Step 5 — Nuts Allocation** Assume the pro-rata rate at snapshot resolves to \~0.5 TREE per Nut (illustrative — actual rate depends on total Final Total Nuts across all eligible wallets and the final size of the TREE allocated for GoNuts S2):

```
Nuts Allocation = 100 (min floor) + (18,960 × 0.5)
                  = 100 + 9,480
                  = 9,580 TREE
```

**Step 6 — Governance**

```
Governance Allocation = 10,000 TREE
```

**Step 7 — NFT**

```
NFT Allocation = 2 × 10,000 = 20,000 TREE
```

**Grand Total**

```
9,580 + 10,000 + 20,000 = 39,580 TREE
```

# FAQ


# Eligibility & Qualification

#### I earned more Nuts after the snapshot. Do they count?

No. Only Nuts accrued up to the snapshot block (25 April 2026, 02:00 UTC) are included. GoNuts writes are paused at the snapshot.

#### I missed the governance vote, can I still qualify?

The governance track is closed at snapshot.&#x20;

#### What if I have fewer than 500 Total Nuts?

You are not eligible for the GoNuts track.&#x20;

#### I voted with a tiny amount of TREE. Does it still count?

Yes. The governance track is binary — if your wallet is recorded as having voted on at least one Treehouse proposal on `treehouse.eth`, you qualify. Voting weight does not affect the allocation.

#### I voted via a delegate wallet address. Does that count?

The wallet that actually submitted the on-chain vote receives the allocation. If you delegated to another wallet, only the delegate's wallet qualifies — not yours.


# Treehouse Squirrel Council

#### Does selling an NFT before the snapshot remove the allocation?

Yes. NFT ownership is determined at the snapshot block — the wallet holding the NFT at that block receives the allocation.

#### My NFT was in an NFT marketplace listing at snapshot. Does it count?

Yes, as long as the NFT is still held by your wallet (not escrowed by the marketplace). Most modern marketplaces like OpenSea Seaport use signature-based listings that keep the NFT in your wallet until sold.

#### My NFT is in a Safe / multisig. Does that wallet qualify?

Yes. The wallet that holds the NFT at the snapshot block receives the allocation, whether it's an EOA or a smart contract wallet.

#### I staked my NFT in a third-party protocol — does it count?

If the NFT was transferred to the staking contract, the staking contract holds it — not you. In that case, your wallet will not receive the NFT allocation. If the staking protocol uses a non-custodial model where the NFT remains in your wallet, it will count.

#### I have 5 NFTs in one wallet and 3 in another. How is this counted?

Each wallet is counted independently. The wallet with 5 NFTs receives 50,000 TREE; the wallet with 3 receives 30,000 TREE. Allocations cannot be combined across wallets.


# Wallets & Claiming

#### I used multiple wallets during the season. Can I combine allocations?

No. Each wallet receives its own independent allocation based on its activity. There is no mechanism to merge or redirect allocations from one wallet to another.

#### Can I claim to a different wallet than the eligible one?

No. The claim must be executed from the wallet on the allocation list. The TREE is sent to the claiming wallet.

#### I used a centralized exchange deposit address for GoNuts activity. Will I receive the airdrop?

Only if you can access and sign transactions from that address — which is generally not possible with CEX deposit addresses. We strongly recommend using a self-custodial wallet for all on-chain activity.

#### Can I claim from a hardware wallet / Ledger / Trezor?

Yes. Any standard Ethereum wallet that can sign transactions works, including hardware wallets.

#### Can I claim from a Safe multisig or smart contract wallet?

Yes. The claim function accepts any msg.sender, so smart contract wallets can claim provided they can execute the claim transaction.

#### Which network is the airdrop on?

Ethereum Mainnet. You will pay standard mainnet gas to claim.

#### How do I claim?

Connect your eligible wallet to the official claim URL, verify your allocation, and sign the claim transaction. The contract will verify your Merkle proof and transfer your unlocked TREE to your wallet.

#### What is a Merkle proof and do I need to generate it myself?

No — the claim UI generates it for you automatically. A Merkle proof is cryptographic data that proves your wallet is on the allocation list without requiring the contract to store the full list on-chain.

#### How long is the claim window?

The claim window closes on **25 November 2026, 07:00 UTC** — approximately 7 months after launch. This gives you the full vesting period plus an additional month to claim your tokens.

#### What happens if I don't claim in time?

Any unclaimed TREE after 25 November 2026 is returned to the Treehouse treasury.&#x20;

20% of your total allocation is unlocked at 29 April 2026, 07:00 UTC. The remaining 80% vests linearly over 180 days, ending approximately 26 October 2026.

No. Vesting begins immediately at launch with no cliff period.

#### Do I claim vested tokens as they unlock, or all at once at the end?

You can claim vested tokens at any time after they unlock. The vesting contract tracks how much is claimable based on elapsed time since launch, if claimed before the full unlock, the unvested portion will be forgone.

#### Can I transfer my vesting position to another wallet?

No. Your vesting is tied to the original claiming wallet. Once TREE is vested and claimed to your wallet, you can transfer it freely like any ERC-20.

#### Can I sell my claimed (unlocked) TREE immediately?

Yes. Any TREE that has been unlocked and claimed to your wallet is standard ERC-20 and freely transferable.

# Pre-Deposit Vaults


# Overview

Pre-Deposit Vaults are on-chain, time-locked staking contracts that allow TREE holders to earn enhanced yield by backing individual DOR Panelists. By depositing into a vault, you are effectively delegating stake to a specific Panelist whose performance—measured by rate forecast accuracy and participation—directly impacts your final yield.

These vaults serve as the first real utility for TREE and offer early contributors a way to align with the protocol’s core mechanism: the Decentralized Offered Rates (DOR) system.

Each Panelist operates a dedicated vault with a fixed cap. Your stake signals conviction in their forecasting ability. At the end of the staking period, yield is assigned based on how well the Panelist performs relative to others.

### Relation to DOR

Pre-Deposit Vaults play a foundational role in bootstrapping the Treehouse DOR network. When Panelists submit rate forecasts for benchmarks like the Treehouse Ethereum Staking Rate (TESR), those submissions are aggregated into consensus rates used across the ecosystem. These vaults help to incentivize early community participation in this process by tying rewards directly to forecasting quality.

### Panelist Selection

Each vault corresponds to a DOR Panelist. Users are encouraged to evaluate each Panelist’s historical performance, accuracy, and consistency before committing capital. Supporting the right Panelist allows you to maximize returns and strengthen data integrity within the Treehouse rate infrastructure.

Learn more about [DOR](https://docs.treehouse.finance/protocol/dor/introduction) and view the latest [published benchmark rates](https://dor.treehouse.finance/).

# Key Features

Panelist Vaults: Each Panelist operates a unique vault, capped at 1.5M TREE.

Fixed-Term Staking: Deposits are time-locked for 9 months (270 days). Early withdrawal will forfeit all accrued rewards.

## Performance-Based APR:&#x20;

Final rewards are based on panelist accuracy rankings:

* 1st: \~75% APR
* 2nd: \~60% APR
* 3rd: \~55% APR
* All others: \~50% APR (guaranteed if staked full term)

{% hint style="info" %}
Note:&#x20;

The above mentioned APRs are an estimate.
{% endhint %}

## Leaderboard Methodology

The Panelists are ranked by the following formula:

$$
mae + mae \times (1 - p)
$$

### Mean Absolute Error (MAE):

Treehouse checks how accurate Panelists' predictions are on a daily basis. Accuracy is scored by measuring the MAE (Mean Absolute Error), which measures how far the Panelist’s predictions are from the average Spot Ethereum Staking Rate published by Treehouse DOR.

$$
mae = \frac{\sum\_{i=1}^{N} \left| R\_i - s\_i \right|}{N}
$$

mae: Mean Absolute Error

Ri:  the average spot DOR value published by Treehouse for day i  (i.e reference rate)

Si: the panelist’s prediction for day i

N:  the total number of predictions submitted during the period

### Participation (p):

The number of predictions a Panelist actually submitted is divided by the number of predictions they were supposed to submit.

$$
p = \frac{\text{Number of predictions submitted}}{\text{Number of predictions expected}}
$$

# Governance

Treehouse DAO is the decentralized governance body of the Treehouse Protocol. TREE and NFT holders can propose, discuss, and vote on changes to parameters, strategies, and treasury management.&#x20;

This community-driven process ensures that Treehouse evolves transparently and sustainably, aligning incentives between users, contributors, and stakeholders while distributing decision-making power across the ecosystem.


# Voting Mechanics

TREE will facilitate the decentralized on-chain governance of Treehouse DAO. Specifically, our voting structure is designed to uphold the core principles of transparency and inclusivity, reflecting our commitment to ensuring that the protocol’s future is shaped collectively and fairly by TREE token holders.

Currently, there are four ways to acquire voting power within the Treehouse DAO:

1. Hold TREE in wallet
2. Hold NFT in wallet (1 NFT = 10,000 TREE in voting power calculations)
3. Deposit TREE in Pre-Deposit Vaults
4. Hold TREE in the airdrop vested program (for the amount that has been vested)

All voting power, represented by veTREE, is calculated using a square-root mechanism: the square root of the token-equivalent amount determines the final voting weight of that component.&#x20;

$$
veTREE = \sqrt{Wallet} + \sqrt{NFT \times 10000} + \sqrt{Vault} + \sqrt{Vested}
$$

This means that the marginal increase of voting power gradually diminishes as the participants’ TREE token holdings increase, ensuring that while large holders have a more significant voice, their influence does not overwhelm the voices of smaller holders.&#x20;

Once a proposal is created, your voting power (veTREE) is fixed based on what you held at that snapshot. If you buy or deposit additional TREE, NFTs, or vested tokens after the proposal’s creation, those new assets won’t count toward your veTREE for that vote.

# Governance Process

Our governance mechanism is designed to ensure a methodical and inclusive decision-making process. To initiate changes or propose improvements within the Treehouse ecosystem, participants must first submit a written Treehouse Improvement Proposal (TIP) through the [Treehouse Governance Forum](https://governance.treehouse.finance/). Once submitted, these proposals undergo an evaluation process as outlined below.

1. Initiate discussion on the [Treehouse Governance Forum](https://governance.treehouse.finance/)&#x20;
2. Admin initiates an official vote on [Snapshot](https://snapshot.box/#/s:treehousefi.eth)
3. Vote timing
   * Vote starts 24 hours after creation on the Treehouse Governance Forum
   * Voting lasts for 72 hours
4. Outcome
   * Passes with simple majority (>50% of votes cast)
   * After the vote closes, the Treehouse team will implement and execute the approved solution

All participants with veTREE are permitted to cast their votes in favor of or against the proposal. Additionally, participants can provide insights and comments, fostering constructive dialogue and community feedback.

With the initiation of the DAO, a set of parameters falls under the community’s jurisdiction for potential change. These parameters include, but are not limited to:

* Approving or removing Panelists who participate in the Treehouse's DOR consensus mechanism.
* Adjusting the minimum veTREE required for participation in the governance participation.
* Deciding on disbursement of funds from the DAO treasury.
* Determining the fees to be received by the DAO for its services.
* Establishing the quorum requirements for governance votes.

Over time, the range of parameters subject to formal governance votes will expand beyond those listed above. DAO members will have the opportunity to propose changes, subject to approval, reflecting a dynamic and community-driven approach to protocol management.
